What The Company Does & Business Model

CarDekho is Jaipur's flagship unicorn with a $1.2B+ valuation. Founded in a small garage in Jaipur in 2008 by brothers Amit & Anurag Jain, it digitizes the entire lifecycle of buying, selling, insuring, and financing vehicles across India and Southeast Asia.

Business Model & Revenue: Comprehensive digital automotive marketplace offering dealer SaaS, car discovery, auto financing & insurance (Rupyy), and B2B auto auctions.
